I Gave My Companies a Memory

For about a year I used AI the way you use a very fast stranger. Brilliant, instant, and starting from zero every single time.

Every session began with the same fifteen minutes of context. Here is what we sell. Here is how the branches are structured. Here is why that number looks wrong but is not. Then the window closed and all of it evaporated.

I assumed the fix was a better model. It was not.

What actually changed

We connected the thing to the systems that hold the truth. The ERP. The operations database. The POS. Not a copy pasted into a prompt — the live thing.

The first time I asked about a stock discrepancy and got an answer that referenced last month’s discrepancy, unprompted, I understood something had shifted.

The model was never the bottleneck. The amnesia was.

The unglamorous part

Almost none of the work was AI work.

It was finding out that grams and g and กรัม were three separate units in our ERP, and that this had quietly broken a fifth of our recipe costings. It was discovering an inventory count that had sat negative for four months because production consumed stock that was never recorded as received.

An agent with a memory does not fix any of that. It surfaces it — loudly, repeatedly, in every answer it gives you. Which is uncomfortable, and correct.

Giving a company a memory means first admitting what the company actually remembers. Most of it is wrong.

What it is worth

The value is not that answers got faster, though they did.

The value is that the questions got better. When you are not spending the first fifteen minutes re-establishing context, you spend them on the second question — the one after the obvious one. That is where anything useful was ever going to be.

What I would tell someone starting

Do not start with the model. Start with what your business already knows, and where that knowledge is written down.

If the answer is in people’s heads and four spreadsheets, you do not have an AI problem yet. You have a bookkeeping problem, and a smarter model will not solve it.

Fix the memory first. The intelligence is the easy part now.
